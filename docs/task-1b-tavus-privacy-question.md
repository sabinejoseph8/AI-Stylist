Subject: Privacy and retention for Free-plan Audio Echo prototype

Hello Tavus support,

I am testing a private AI stylist prototype on the Free plan. OpenAI generates the voice, and Tavus Audio Echo renders a stock avatar. The Echo PAL has no TTS, LLM or STT layer, microphone input is disabled, and conversation recording and automatic recording are set to false. So far, I have used only a fixed synthetic sentence.

Before enabling a real person's microphone, could you confirm in writing:

1. What audio, generated video, transcripts, app-message content and service logs are retained for this Audio Echo path, for how long, and whether backups have a different deletion schedule?
2. Whether submitted or generated content is used for training, evaluation or service improvement on the Free plan, and which opt-out or contractual protections are available?
3. What ending a conversation, deleting it, hard deleting it and requesting account-wide deletion remove, including logs, recordings, transcripts and backups, and the deletion times for each?
4. Which processors and processing locations apply, including Daily, which recording permissions apply to the room/token, and whether your Data Management Policy or an applicable data-processing agreement can be provided?

Please distinguish Free-plan Audio Echo behavior from your consumer services and paid or enterprise plans.

Thank you,
Sabine
