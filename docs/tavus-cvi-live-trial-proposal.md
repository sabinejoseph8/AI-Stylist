Unknown body/auth/correlation, timeout or unexpected greeting ends the attempt before any recommendation. 2. Say an outdoor November wedding brief with emerald green, long sleeves and a USD 500 item-only maximum. Observe app-owned tentative notes and explicitly confirm them. Do not infer a season. Show/speak only the exact synthetic reply checked against the current confirmed revision.
3. Correct the budget to USD 350. Confirm the correction and verify the next checked output uses that revision. While an approved synthetic reply is playing, interrupt once and check whether pending/unsent old output is canceled. Do not promise a downstream flush or exact heard position; any observed stale output is a failure.
4. Inject one deterministic checker rejection or timeout after a new request. Require no unchecked recommendation to be displayed or spoken. Observe whether Tavus emits its own generated fallback. Any invented recommendation/fallback fails the trial and ends the room; do not retry to obtain a pass.
5. End before the app deadline or at the first failure. Confirm device media stops, request provider termination, inspect its bounded status and preserve the complete attempt outcome. Unknown cleanup remains held. Reconcile displayed usage later without refunding the reservation.
