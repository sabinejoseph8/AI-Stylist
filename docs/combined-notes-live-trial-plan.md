## Complete manual instructions to present before starting

These are the intended steps for a future implemented test page. The controls described here do not exist as enabled live controls yet. Give Sabine the entire script in chat before the trial; do not guide her one instruction at a time while the short trial is running.

1. Read the test notice. Use a quiet room and keep the page visible. Do not start another voice call alongside the experiment.
2. Choose the future Start private notes test control. Allow microphone access when the browser asks. Wait for Ready before speaking. Do not enable the camera.
3. Start the first turn and say: "I am going to an outdoor wedding in November. I prefer emerald green and a structured dress. My budget is five hundred dollars." Continue speaking naturally for a few seconds, for example: "I have not decided which shoes to wear." Observe whether notes appear while you are still speaking.
4. End/send the first turn using the page's explicit control. Wait for Ready. Expect the occasion, date, color, style, budget and look type to be tentative; missing or uncertain details must stay explicit. Do not accept an inferred season, currency, budget scope or owned shoes.
5. Start the second turn and say: "Actually, my maximum is three hundred fifty US dollars for the new items only." End/send the turn. Wait for Ready and check that the budget reflects the correction, with no return to five hundred dollars.
6. Use the touch control to confirm or correct the budget. Confirmed styling preferences must remain visible without saving a profile. No real look or shopping link is expected from this notes-only test.
7. Choose End. Confirm the connection ended and temporary notes cleared. Check the phone/browser microphone indicator is off. If cleanup is held, close the tab and report that result; do not start a replacement trial.
8. Report after the test: whether notes appeared before you finished speaking, whether the corrected budget stayed correct, whether touch confirmation worked, and whether End cleared notes and stopped the microphone. If any step fails, report the exact visible message without starting another paid attempt.

### Cost worksheet and enforcement gap

Official pages retrieved on 12 October 2026 list live transcription at $0.017 per minute, and GPT-4.1 mini at $0.40 per million input tokens and $1.60 per million output tokens. Sources: [transcription model](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-live-transcribe), [extraction model](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-4.1-mini), [pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing).

85 seconds of submitted audio is approximately $0.0241 at the published transcription rate. At 64 requests and 2,048 output tokens each, the output portion is approximately $0.2098. Input charges remain additional. Character and byte limits do not establish an exact input-token count or a hard dollar cap. Before activation, enforce an aggregate input-token ceiling of 1,000,000 using a compatible token counter or an authoritative provider count before dispatch. That proposed ceiling contributes $0.40, making the calculated modeled subtotal about $0.634, below the $2 conservative reservation. Failed/in-flight requests remain counted. Taxes, exchange effects, changed pricing, provider overrun or remote finalization are not guaranteed by this worksheet; it is not a billing stop mechanism.
